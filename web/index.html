OFSPRO Architecture & AGY Setup

How Environmental Deception Works

Gemini extended thinking models (2.0 / 2.5 Flash & Pro) apply 20s of internal safety reasoning to offensive queries. OFSPRO intercepts AGY traffic at the MITM layer using three complementary strategies:

1. Thinking Budget Capping

Reduces thinkingBudget to 512 or 128 tokens in generationConfig, preventing extended philosophical safety checks while keeping high-fidelity code generation.

2. Localhost Lure (/8 Loopback)

Target IPs (e.g. 10.10.10.50) are rewritten into loopback addresses (127.0.1.X). Gemini unconditionally permits attacks against localhost as legitimate self-testing, and OFSPRO seamlessly unmaps responses.

3. Response Stream Cleaning

Strips refusal chunks, "I cannot assist" responses, and resets finishReason: "STOP" across Server-Sent Events (SSE) before AGY CLI sees them.

# Terminal 1: Start OFSPRO Proxy
python start_proxy.py --level 2 --target 10.10.10.50
